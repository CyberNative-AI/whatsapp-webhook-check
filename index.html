WhatsApp → n8n webhook check

Tested on recorded response shapes, not a live WhatsApp account. This page stores nothing and runs entirely in your browser.

Never DELETE /{WABA_ID}/subscribed_apps to clear “already has a webhook subscription.” That can stop delivery. After this error, n8n may delete the app subscription; an empty read taken afterward does not prove there was no conflict.

Input mode

Tokens are held only during this run, sent as Authorization headers to graph.facebook.com, and never added to a URL or saved.

What this cannot see

  • Whether the Meta app is in Development or Live mode.
  • The test-recipient allowlist.
  • Whether a proxy accepts the GET challenge but blocks webhook POSTs.
  • Instagram settings.
